# Licensing Information

FLUX is dual-licensed to offer flexibility for both open-source and commercial use cases. You can choose the license that best fits your project's needs.

---

## 1. Open Source Use (GPL v3)

For open-source projects, academic use, evaluation, or personal hobby work, FLUX is available under the **GNU General Public License version 3 (GPL v3)**.

- **Requirements**: If you integrate FLUX into your software, your entire application must also be open-sourced under a GPL-compatible license. You must share your source code when redistributing your application.
- **Full License Text**: See [LICENSE-GPL.txt](LICENSE-GPL.txt).

---

## 2. Commercial Use (Proprietary License)

If you are developing a commercial application, proprietary product, or closed-source system and do not wish to release your application's source code under the GPL v3 copyleft requirements, you must purchase a **FLUX Commercial License**.

- **Benefits**:
  - Allows integration into proprietary, closed-source products.
  - Removes copyleft obligations.
  - Custom SLA, professional support, and maintenance options are available.
- **License Terms & Contact**: See [LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.txt](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.txt) for template terms.
- **Inquiries**: Please contact [licensing@flux-compress.org](mailto:licensing@flux-compress.org) to obtain a license.

---

## Which license should I use?

| Your Use Case | Recommended License |
| :--- | :--- |
| Personal evaluation, hobby projects, or open-source GPL v3 projects | **GPL v3** (Free) |
| Proprietary, closed-source commercial software | **Commercial License** (Paid) |
| SaaS backend (where source code is not distributed to users) | **GPL v3** (Free, but check your legal team) |
| Custom SDK/redistribution as a compiler or compression library tool | **Commercial License** (Paid) |
